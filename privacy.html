Privacy Policy — GG Khatabook

Last updated: October 15, 2026

GG Khatabook ("the App") is a digital ledger/khata application that helps shopkeepers keep track of customer accounts. This page explains what data the App collects and how it is used.

What data we collect

  • Your ledger records (customer names, amounts, dates) — this data is stored locally on your own device.
  • At your request, for backup purposes, the same records are saved to your own Google Drive (signed in with your Google account). We do not receive or read these files.
  • Licence information on Firebase Firestore (Google Cloud): a random app User ID created on your device, the free-trial start date and your subscription expiry date. This is used only to keep your subscription working on reinstall.
  • When you connect Google Drive, Google sign-in (Firebase Authentication) is used; your Google email is used only to keep backups on the same account.

Google Drive access

This app uses Google's drive.file permission (scope). This means:

  • The App can only see or write files that it has created itself.
  • The App does not have access to any other files, folders, or data in your Google Drive.
  • This access is used only to create backup files inside a single folder named "GG Khatabook Backup".

Where your data is stored

  • Primary data: locally on your own device's storage.
  • Backup copies: in your own Google Drive account.
  • Licence information (User ID, trial date, subscription expiry): Firebase Firestore (Google Cloud).
  • All data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS).
  • We do not sell or share this data with any third party.

Subscriptions and payments

  • In the Google Play version, subscriptions (1 month / 1 year) are purchased only through Google Play Billing. We never see or store your card or payment details.
  • To confirm a purchase, the purchase token is checked with Google Play by our verification server. No other personal data is sent.
  • You can cancel anytime from Google Play → Payments & subscriptions.

Fingerprint login

Fingerprint / screen-lock login uses your phone's own security system. Your fingerprint never leaves your device and is never sent to us.

Deleting your data — GG Khatabook

Steps to delete your GG Khatabook data:

  1. Data on your phone: uninstall the App, or clear its storage (Settings → Apps → GG Khatabook → Storage → Clear data).
  2. Backup in your Google Drive: this is in your own Drive and we cannot access it. Open Google Drive and delete the folder "GG Khatabook Backup".
  3. Licence record (App User ID, trial date, subscription expiry): email pbtcodelabs@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my data" and your App User ID (shown in the App). We will delete it within 7 days.

Nothing else is kept by us. Purchase records of Google Play subscriptions are kept by Google Play under Google's own policy.
