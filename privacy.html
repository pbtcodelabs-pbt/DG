Privacy Policy — DG Khatabook

Last updated: August 24, 2026

DG Khatabook ("the App") is a digital ledger/khata application that helps shopkeepers keep track of customer accounts. This page explains what data the App collects and how it is used.

What data we collect

  • Your ledger records (customer names, amounts, dates) — this data is stored locally on your own device.
  • At your request, for backup purposes, the same records are also saved to your own Google Drive and to Firebase Firestore (Google Cloud).

Google Drive access

This app uses Google's drive.file permission (scope). This means:

  • The App can only see or write files that it has created itself.
  • The App does not have access to any other files, folders, or data in your Google Drive.
  • This access is used only to create backup files inside a single folder named "DG Khatabook Backups".

Where your data is stored

  • Primary data: locally on your own device's storage.
  • Backup copies: in your own Google Drive account, and in Firebase Firestore (Google Cloud).
  • We do not sell or share this data with any third party.

Deleting your data

You can delete the local data at any time by uninstalling the App from your device. To delete backup copies stored in Drive or Firestore, please contact us using the email below.
