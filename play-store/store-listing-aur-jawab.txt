Full description (انگلش):
GG Khatabook is a simple and fast digital khata (ledger) for shopkeepers and small businesses in Pakistan.

Keep every customer's udhaar (credit) and payments in one place — no more paper registers.

KEY FEATURES
• Add customers with name and phone number
• Record "Received" and "Paid" amounts in one tap
• See each customer's running balance and full history
• Total received, paid and net balance on the home screen
• Share colourful receipts and reminders on WhatsApp and SMS
• Print or save customer reports
• Automatic backup to your own Google Drive (morning and after 7 PM)
• One-tap restore on a new phone
• PIN lock and fingerprint login to keep your khata private
• Urdu-friendly design with Nastaleeq receipts
• Works offline — your data stays on your phone

SUBSCRIPTION
Start with a 3-day free trial. Then choose a Google Play subscription:
• 1 Month
• 1 Year (best value)
Cancel anytime from Google Play → Payments & subscriptions.

Your data is yours: records are stored on your device and backed up only to your own Google Drive.
